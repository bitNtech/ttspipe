1 · Active voice

POST /v1/voice — every WebSocket turn that names no voice of its own uses this one, until you replace or clear it. The transcript must be the clip's exact words: a wrong one is not an error, just a worse voice.

2 · Speak — WebSocket

ws /v1/tts/live — text in as you produce it, audio out as it decodes. The started frame reports which voice_id you actually got. pcm and wav play live through the Web Audio API, buffered just deeply enough to play without breaking up; mp3 and flac need a decoder, so they are collected and played at the end.

3 · One-shot clone — HTTP

POST /v1/tts/clone — clones the clip attached to this one request and ignores the active voice entirely. Useful for trying a clip out before committing to it above.
